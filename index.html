About Thermalair Inc.

Mechanical systems that carry a building’s performance — and its energy bill.

Heating, ventilation, air conditioning, and refrigeration consume 40–60% of a building’s energy. That makes mechanical engineering one of the most consequential decisions in any project. Thermalair works with owners and integrated design teams to translate business goals into measurable building performance — weighing first cost, operating cost, capital expenditure, and LEED® cost/benefit from day one.

Our engineers and skilled technicians then evaluate design options, coordinate in BIM, fabricate, install, and service the systems that keep facilities running efficiently for decades.

  • Integrated design & value engineering with owners and architects
  • Union workforce — UA Local 250 & SMART Local 105
  • Licensed in California (C-4, C-20, C-38) and Nevada (L-39)
  • Insured & bonded — Travelers / Liberty Mutual

Core Services

Three disciplines. One accountable partner.

From first load calculation to 24/7 emergency response, Thermalair covers the full lifecycle of commercial mechanical systems.

01

Construction Services

Fundamental to every building project are its mechanical systems. Thermalair quantifies what owners actually need — comfort, capacity, and lifecycle cost — then engineers and builds systems designed to lower costs over the life of the building, not just on day one.

  • Design-build & plan-spec
  • Boilers & hot water / steam
  • Refrigeration systems
  • Clean-room HVAC
  • LEED®-focused delivery
02

Engineering & BIM Modelling

At the forefront of technology for design, pre-construction trade coordination, construction layout, and product fabrication. Our team routinely performs load calculations, equipment selection, and performance-based energy modelling — coordinated in Revit BIM before a single duct is hung.

  • Load calculations
  • Equipment selection
  • Energy modelling
  • Revit BIM modelling
  • CFD analysis
  • Clash detection
  • Robotic layout
